Event · Coffee & Calling

Aligning Your Business With God

God has given us talents. On Sunday 10 January 2027 we come together in Amsterdam to discover how to put those talents where they bear the most fruit. With dinner, worship, a sermon on technology and AI, and plenty of room for conversation.

Sunday 10 January 2027 18:00 to 22:00 Amsterdam

Why this evening

God has given each of us talents. Our purpose is to find the right posture, so those talents end up in the place where they render the most value.

Think of a singer during worship. One voice can light up a whole room, carrying the fruits of the Lord to a larger audience. The same is true for what we build.

As God-led Christians in tech, we can reflect much of the light we receive from our intimate relationship with God through technology.

Right now, technology is mostly shaped by a handful of giants. We believe Christians should come together and create more real estate for God’s presence, in the digital world too. Coffee & Calling is a place to think, pray and build toward that together.

For founders, developers, designers and anyone who works in or with technology and takes their faith seriously. But this evening is not only for Christians. If you feel drawn to it, or you are still exploring, you are warmly welcome. Feel free to send us a message.
