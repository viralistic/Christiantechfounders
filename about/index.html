Builders who put God first

Christian Tech Founders brings Christ-led founders and builders in tech together, so nobody has to carry their calling alone.

2025
[01] How it started

Two worlds that did not understand each other.

The idea for CTF came from a simple observation. In church, the entrepreneurial and technical side of people was not really understood. In the tech world, deeper meaning was not valued.

The first gathering in 2025 drew around forty people. Too many, too mixed in level and too shallow to go deep. That was the lesson learned.

Since April 2026 we work in smaller groups around real projects, next to evenings like Coffee & Calling that are open to everyone who feels drawn to them.

[02] What we believe

What drives us

  • 01Excellence and meaning belong together
  • 02What we build shapes people and culture
  • 03Responsibility comes with power
  • 04The right people in one place speed each other up

We believe a calling from God means standing where your talents fit best. There, like a kaleidoscope, you shine His light onto as many people as possible, as strongly as possible.
