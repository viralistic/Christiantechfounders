You don’t have to carry it alone.

We know what it feels like to stand between two worlds: deep in faith and deep in technology. As if no one fully understands what you see.

Christian Tech Founders exists to bring Christian ideas to life with the technology they need, and to let you know: you are not the only one. There are others who feel the same tension, ask the same questions, and want to build the same things.

How we do it

Three ways we bring Christian ideas and tech together.

01 / Connect
Find like-minded people

Connect with others who take both faith and technology seriously. People who understand what you feel and see.

02 / Build
Turn ideas into action

Work together on projects that reflect Christian values and make a real impact on society.

03 / Grow
Learn from each other

Sharpen your thinking, your faith and your technical skills in a community that takes all three seriously.
