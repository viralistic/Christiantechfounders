[01] Sound familiar?

Building alone is harder than it should be.

You love God and you love building. But church friends do not always get startups, and tech peers do not always get your faith. So the big questions stay with you. Is my company part of my calling? Am I using my gifts where God wants them?

Deadlines, metrics and worries keep pulling your eyes to a lower place. Meanwhile a few giants shape the technology the whole world uses, and Christian builders stay scattered.

[02] Why we exist

We know the tension. We live it too.

We know what it feels like to stand between two worlds, deep in faith and deep in technology, as if no one fully understands what you see.

We believe a calling from God means standing in the place that lines up as closely as possible with your talents. There are thousands of things you could do. But when you stand where your gifts fit, you shine His light like a kaleidoscope, onto as many people as possible, as strongly as possible.

That is why we started Christian Tech Founders: to bring Christ-led founders, developers and designers together around dinner, worship and honest conversation.
