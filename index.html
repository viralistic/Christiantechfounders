Sound familiar?

Building alone is harder than it should be.

You love God and you love building. But church friends do not always get startups, and tech peers do not always get your faith. So the big questions stay with you. Is my company part of my calling? Am I using my gifts where God wants them?

Deadlines, metrics and worries keep pulling your eyes to a lower place. Meanwhile a few giants shape the technology the whole world uses, and Christian builders stay scattered. The talents God gave us end up working in the wrong places, or not at all.

Why we exist

We know the tension. We live it too.

We know what it feels like to stand between two worlds, deep in faith and deep in technology, as if no one fully understands what you see.

We believe a calling from God means standing in the place that lines up as closely as possible with your talents. There are thousands of things you could do. But when you stand where your gifts fit, you shine His light like a kaleidoscope, onto as many people as possible, as strongly as possible.

That is why we started Christian Tech Founders. We bring Christian founders, developers and designers together around dinner, worship and honest conversation, so you can find your people and take your next step. Our evenings, called Coffee & Calling, start in Amsterdam.

What we do

Everything is built around one goal: aligning your business with God.

Sunday evenings
Worship and pray together

Worship lifts your eyes from the low things that steal your attention. It moves us to compassion, and from there to mission and serving. We explore that together, and pray for each other and our work.

Sermons
AI, technology and God’s real estate

Honest teaching on AI and technology, how they shape a world that is often dark, and how we can claim more space for God’s presence in it.

Talent alignment
Find where your gifts fit

In a talent alignment session you discover where your skills and passions have the most impact, so you can align your business with God instead of guessing.

Projects
Get help to build

Bring your project. We help you move it forward and publish it on our site, so you can find funding and people who want to build with you.

How it works

Three simple steps from building alone to building together.

Step 01
Sign up for an evening

Pick the next Coffee & Calling and reserve your seat on Luma. It takes a minute.

Step 02
Meet your people

Share dinner, worship together and talk with founders who understand both your faith and your work.

Step 03
Take your next step

Leave with new friends, fresh perspective and a clearer sense of where your talents belong.
