# Christian Events in Amsterdam: Where Believers Meet

Christian events in Amsterdam range from Sunday services in English and worship nights to Bible studies, conferences and gatherings for Christian founders. The easiest way to find them is through church websites, Christian event calendars and community evenings like Coffee & Calling, where believers in tech meet over dinner and worship.

Amsterdam has a reputation for many things, and faith is not usually the first one. Search for events in Amsterdam and you will mostly find festivals, exhibitions and nightlife. But underneath that, the city has a growing Christian community: international churches, young believers, worship nights and people who want to bring their faith into their work.

The problem is not that Christian events in Amsterdam do not exist. It is that they are scattered and hard to find, especially if you are new to the city or do not speak Dutch.

## What kinds of Christian events are there in Amsterdam?

Christian events in Amsterdam fall into five broad types: church services, worship nights, small groups, conferences and themed gatherings for specific groups, like founders, students or creatives.

1. **Church services.** Several churches hold services in English, which makes them a natural first step for internationals. Liberty Church Amsterdam is one example.
2. **Worship nights.** Evenings built around open worship and prayer, often across churches.
3. **Bible studies and small groups.** Weekly groups that meet in homes, cafés or church buildings.
4. **Conferences and seminars.** Day events on topics like leadership, mission or prayer.
5. **Community gatherings.** Events for people who share a calling or profession, such as Christian entrepreneurs and builders in tech.

## How do you find Christian events in Amsterdam?

You find Christian events in Amsterdam by combining three sources: church websites, Christian event calendars and word of mouth from local believers.

- **Church websites.** Most churches list their services, worship nights and courses on their own site.
- **Christian event calendars.** National agendas such as [EventsForChrist.nl](https://eventsforchrist.nl/) collect concerts, worship evenings and conferences across the Netherlands, including Amsterdam.
- **Eventbrite and Luma.** Many smaller gatherings, including ours, use platforms like Eventbrite or Luma. Search for "christian" or "worship" with Amsterdam as the location.
- **People.** The best events often spread through friends. Go to one gathering and you usually hear about three more.

## What should you expect at a Christian event in Amsterdam?

Expect a mix of people. Amsterdam's Christian community is international, so many events run in English or switch between English and Dutch. You will meet locals, expats, students and professionals.

Most gatherings are welcoming to newcomers and to people who are still exploring faith. At Coffee & Calling, for example, you do not need to be a Christian to come. If you feel drawn to the evening, you are welcome.

## Why go to Christian events at all?
