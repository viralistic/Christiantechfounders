Christian Events in the Netherlands: Festivals, Conferences and Gatherings

2 Nov 20264 min readChristian Tech Founders

Christian events in the Netherlands include large festivals and conferences, worship nights, concerts and smaller gatherings for specific groups like founders, students and creatives. The big ones cluster around Ascension, Pentecost and the summer. Smaller events happen every week across the country, and Christian event calendars help you find them.

The Netherlands is often called one of the most secular countries in Europe. Yet the Christian event calendar is full. From festivals with thousands of visitors to evenings with twenty people around one table, there is more happening than most people realise.

This guide walks through the main types of Christian events, a few well-known names, where to find what is on, and how to choose an event that actually helps you grow.

What types of Christian events are there in the Netherlands?

Christian events in the Netherlands come in five main types: festivals, conferences, worship nights, concerts and community gatherings.

  1. Festivals. Multi-day events with worship, teaching, seminars and often camping.
  2. Conferences. One or more days of teaching on a theme like leadership, mission, prayer or the Holy Spirit.
  3. Worship nights. Evenings centred on open worship and prayer, often organised by churches together.
  4. Concerts. Christian artists and choirs, especially around Christmas and Easter.
  5. Community gatherings. Smaller events for people who share a life stage or calling, such as Christian entrepreneurs, students or singles.

Which big Christian events happen every year?

The best-known Christian events in the Netherlands are the large festivals around Christian holidays and the summer. Check the official websites for exact dates, because they change every year.

  • Opwekking Pinksterconferentie. One of the largest Christian gatherings in the country, held over the Pentecost weekend in Biddinghuizen. More information on pinksterconferentie.nl.
  • Strandheem. A festival around Ascension Day with services, seminars and worship.
  • Graceland Festival. A Christian festival in the summer.
  • New Wine. A well-known Christian conference and festival movement that also gathers in the Netherlands.

Next to these, denominations and networks run their own events. The Protestantse Kerk, for example, lists lectures, webinars and meeting days on its own events page.

Where do you find Christian events in the Netherlands?

You find Christian events in the Netherlands through Christian event calendars, ticket platforms and the websites of churches and organisations.

  • EventsForChrist.nl collects worship evenings, concerts, festivals, conferences and youth events in one agenda.
  • Christelijke Concertagenda focuses on Christian concerts across the country.
  • TrueTickets sells tickets for Christian concerts, theatre and conferences.
  • Eventbrite and Luma host many smaller gatherings. Search for "christelijk" or "christian" plus your city.
  • Church and network websites list their own events and often those of partners.

How do you choose the right Christian event?

Choose a Christian event by asking what you need right now: inspiration, teaching, worship or people who walk the same road.

Big festivals are great for inspiration and worship with thousands of others. Conferences help when you want to go deep on one theme. But if you feel alone in your calling, a smaller gathering is often more valuable. Around one table, people actually get to know you, pray with you and remember your name next time.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest Christian event in the Netherlands?

The Opwekking Pinksterconferentie is one of the largest Christian gatherings in the Netherlands. It takes place every year over the Pentecost weekend in Biddinghuizen.

Where can I find a Christian events agenda?

Use national calendars like EventsForChrist.nl and the Christelijke Concertagenda, ticket platforms like TrueTickets, and Eventbrite or Luma for smaller gatherings.
